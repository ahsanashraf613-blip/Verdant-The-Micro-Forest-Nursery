Plant in an afternoon.
Thrives for decades.

6 simple steps – or let our free AR app guide you.

1

Choose your kit & zone

Select USDA hardiness zone – we curate native species for your area. No invasive ever.

2

Receive your kit

Box includes bare‑root plants, planting map, cardboard weed barrier, compressed leaf mulch, and QR code to AR app.

3

Prepare the ground

Lay cardboard, wet it. No tilling, no rototiller. Kills grass naturally.

4

Arrange plants (AR or map)

Point phone at ground – AR overlays exact spots. Or use 1:1 scale map. Dense random pattern (3-5 plants/m²).

5

Plant & mulch

Cut X in cardboard, dig small hole, insert plant, backfill with native soil, spread 4" mulch.

6

Water-in & walk away

Soak once. Water every 3-4 days for first 30 days if no rain. After that – zero watering (except extreme drought).
