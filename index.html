6 simple steps – or let our free AR app guide you.

1

Choose your kit & zone

Select USDA hardiness zone – we curate native species for your area. No invasive ever.

2

Receive your kit

Box includes bare‑root plants, planting map, cardboard weed barrier, compressed leaf mulch, and QR code to AR app.

3

Prepare the ground

Lay cardboard, wet it. No tilling, no rototiller. Kills grass naturally.

4

Arrange plants (AR or map)

Point phone at ground – AR overlays exact spots. Or use 1:1 scale map. Dense random pattern (3-5 plants/m²).

5

Plant & mulch

Cut X in cardboard, dig small hole, insert plant, backfill with native soil, spread 4" mulch.

6

Water-in & walk away

Soak once. Water every 3-4 days for first 30 days if no rain. After that – zero watering (except extreme drought).

The science of tiny, fast forests

Three decades of research prove: ultra‑dense native planting restores ecosystems in 2 years, not 50.

History of the Miyawaki Method

1970s

Dr. Akira Miyawaki develops method in Japan

1990s

First international projects in Brazil and Europe

2015

First US pilot in California

2024

Verdant launches first consumer kits

📈 10x faster growth

Schall et al. 2022: Dense planting (3 plants/m²) increases biomass accumulation 7.2x vs single-tree planting. Verdant pilots: 1.6m avg height in 18 months vs 0.2m for controls.

🦋 30x more biodiversity

Miyawaki 2004: After 10 years, Miyawaki forests support 30x more bird species than adjacent lawns. Our Portland pilot recorded 12 bird species in year 2.

🌱 Soil carbon sequestration

Lal 2018: Converting lawn to dense native planting increases soil organic carbon by 1.2 tons/ha/year. A 100 sq ft Verdant forest sequesters ~290 lbs CO₂ per year total.
